# cbssuitabilityhaiti: Sanitation Zoning and Water Points, Cap Haitien, Haiti 2015-2022

This package contains data for a sanitation zoning assessment of Cap
Haitien, Haiti, used for an analysis of the suitability of
container-based sanitation (CBS). It combines two datasets:
neighbourhood level zoning data from a 2022 sanitation zoning
assessment, covering Cap Haitien and five other communes in Haiti, and
the locations of 1849 water points in the Cap Haitien area from the
mWater platform, added between 2015 and 2022.
